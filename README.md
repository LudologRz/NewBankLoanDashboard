### Bank Loan Dashboard

A fully interactive **Bank Loan Dashboard** for analyzing loan applications, funded amounts, repayment status, and key financial metrics for American banks. This dashboard provides deep insights into loan performance and borrower profiles through a variety of visualizations and filters.

## Overview

The dashboard provides a high-level and granular view of loan performance, including:

- **Total loan applications**
- **Total funded amount**
- **Total amount received**
- **Average interest rate**
- **Average debt-to-income (DTI) ratio**
- **Month-to-date (MTD) and month-over-month (MoM) metrics** for all key indicators

The insights are derived from a dataset of **American bank loans** and allow users to explore trends and patterns in lending and repayment.

---

## Features

- Interactive, filterable visualizations
- Insights at both high-level summary and detailed granularity
- Comparison of loan performance by status, term, purpose, and borrower demographics
- Easy export of metrics and visual insights

---

## Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following interactive charts:

### Overview Charts

- **Pie Charts**:  
  - Ratio of good vs. bad loans issued  
  - Ratio of total funded amount by loan term (36 vs. 60 months)

- **Map Chart**:  
  - Total funded amount by state (U.S.)

- **Area Chart**:  
  - Total funded amount by month

### Detailed Insights

- **Bar Charts**:  
  - Total funded amount by loan purpose  
  - Total funded amount by employee length  

- **Tree Map**:  
  - Total funded amount by home ownership

## Filters

Users can interactively filter the dashboard by:

- Loan type (two types available)
- Verification status
- Employee length
- Loan grade
- Loan purpose

These filters update **all charts and metrics in real time**, allowing detailed exploration of trends and patterns.
